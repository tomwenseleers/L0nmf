# L0nmf 0.1.0
* Coupled multisample NMF shares spectra across aligned, sample-specific log-concave profiles on irregular original grids, with disk-backed observations and all seven signal families.
* Batched native spectral scores, shared row weights/offsets and cached grouping geometry reduce repeated work in large multichannel fits.

* Original-channel completion checks conditional deletions, local profile/spectral fusions and missed residual peaks, with complete-likelihood safeguards and native local updates.
* Sparse log-concave NMF with local profile and spectral updates, rank selection and full-channel refinement.
* Flexible noise families, pooled dispersion estimates, PSF calibration and blind deconvolution.
* Experimental chromatogram datasets and simulation tools.
